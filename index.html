Why choose home visit podiatry?

For many patients, getting to a clinic is the hardest part. We remove that barrier entirely.

HCPC Registered

Fully registered with the Health and Care Professions Council. Your safety is our first concern at every visit.

Care at Home

No parking, no waiting rooms, no difficult journeys. Particularly beneficial for elderly patients, those with reduced mobility, or anyone who finds travel difficult.

One-to-One Care

Your appointment is yours. No rushing, no queues, no distractions. Every visit is focused entirely on you and your needs.

What we treat

From routine maintenance to specialist diabetic care, all in the comfort of your home.

General Foot Care

Routine nail cutting, nail thinning for thickened or difficult nails, and general foot hygiene. Ideal for elderly patients or anyone who struggles to manage their own foot care safely.

From £45 / visit

Diabetic Foot Assessment

A thorough assessment of circulation and sensation for patients with diabetes. Early detection of changes reduces the risk of serious complications, and regular reviews are an essential part of diabetic foot health.

From £55 / assessment

Corns, Callus and Verruca

Treatment of corns and callus (hard skin) using professional debridement techniques. Verruca treatment including appropriate clinical management. Relief is often felt immediately after treatment.

From £50 / visit

Ingrown Toenail Treatment

Conservative treatment of ingrown toenails to relieve pain and prevent infection. Advice on correct cutting technique and footwear to reduce the chance of recurrence. Nail surgery referral arranged if required.

From £55 / visit
