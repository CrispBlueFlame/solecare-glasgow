A personal, professional service

Solecare Glasgow East was founded to bring qualified podiatry care directly to patients who need it most, at home, without the barriers of travel or clinic waiting times.

As a HCPC-registered podiatrist with many years of clinical experience, I understand that foot health is not always straightforward, and that for many patients, accessing care can be as much of a challenge as the condition itself. The home visit model removes that barrier entirely.

Whether you are managing a long-term condition such as diabetes, dealing with a painful corn or ingrown nail, or simply finding routine foot care increasingly difficult to manage on your own, I bring the same standard of care to your door as you would expect in any clinical setting, but in the comfort and convenience of your own home.

I am particularly experienced in working with older patients and those with complex health needs. Every appointment is relaxed, unhurried, and focused entirely on you.

What to expect at your first visit

There is nothing to prepare beyond a clean, accessible space to sit. Here is how a typical appointment works.

Initial consultation

We begin with a brief chat about your foot health history, any current concerns, and relevant medical conditions. This helps ensure treatment is tailored to you.

Assessment and treatment

Both feet are assessed and treatment carried out during the same appointment. Everything is explained as we go, so you always know what is being done and why.

Advice and follow-up

At the end of your appointment, you will receive clear advice on self-care between visits and a recommendation for follow-up timing if relevant. No jargon, just plain guidance.
