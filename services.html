Our Services

Professional podiatry at home across Glasgow East

General Foot Care and Nail Cutting

Routine nail cutting and care carried out safely and professionally. This is one of the most requested services for elderly patients or anyone who finds it difficult to reach or maintain their own foot hygiene.

We also address thickened or difficult nails using professional thinning and reduction techniques, leaving nails comfortable and correctly shaped.

  • Nail cutting and shaping
  • Nail thinning and reduction for thickened nails
  • Foot hygiene advice
  • Footwear guidance where relevant
From £45 / visit (approx. 30 minutes)

Diabetic Foot Assessment

Patients with diabetes are at increased risk of foot complications, including ulcers, infection, and reduced circulation. Regular professional assessments are a vital part of managing this risk.

Our diabetic foot assessment checks circulation, sensation, skin condition, and nail health. You will receive a clear summary of findings and any recommendations at the end of each visit.

  • Circulation and sensation screening
  • Skin and nail inspection
  • Risk stratification
  • Written care recommendations
  • Referral to GP or specialist if needed
From £55 / assessment (approx. 45 minutes)

Corns, Callus and Verruca Treatment

Corns and callus (areas of hard, thickened skin) develop in response to pressure and friction. While they are not dangerous, they can be uncomfortable and, for patients with diabetes or poor circulation, can become a more serious issue if left unmanaged.

Verruca (plantar warts) are viral and can be persistent. We provide appropriate clinical management and will advise on the most suitable approach for your situation.

  • Professional debridement of corns and callus
  • Immediate relief in most cases
  • Verruca assessment and treatment
  • Padding and pressure-relief advice
  • Footwear and self-care guidance
From £50 / visit (approx. 30–45 minutes)

Ingrown Toenail Treatment

An ingrown toenail occurs when the edge of the nail grows into the surrounding skin, causing pain, swelling, and sometimes infection. Conservative treatment at home can provide significant relief and, for many patients, prevents the need for surgical intervention.

If conservative treatment is insufficient and nail surgery is required, we will arrange an appropriate referral on your behalf.

  • Careful removal of the offending nail spike
  • Wound care and infection management if needed
  • Advice on correct cutting technique
  • Footwear guidance to reduce recurrence
  • Referral for nail surgery if appropriate
From £55 / visit (approx. 45 minutes)
